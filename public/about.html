CONCEPT

Built like standing at the bus stop.

Most bus apps make you work for the answer: find your stop on a map, find the bus number for that route, then find the arrival time for that number. Three lookups before you know anything.

SGBusNavi flips that around. It finds the bus stop nearest you automatically, and shows every bus arriving there right now — sorted by time, badge front and center — exactly the way you'd read the buses rolling in if you were standing there yourself.

No searching. No scrolling through timetables. Just look, and know.

FEATURES

A few things it does really well

🏠

Everything, one screen

Your saved stops, a live map, and every arriving bus — all on the Home screen the moment you open the app. No digging through tabs to get the answer.

🚌

See how close, not just a number

A live progress bar shows exactly where each bus is on its way to you, so you can tell at a glance whether to walk faster or take your time.

🗺️

Full route, one tap

Tap "Route" on any bus to see the live map and the complete list of stops in order — including every MRT station it passes along the way.

⭐

Save your places

Save the stops you use often — home, office, the gym — with your own icon, colour, and title, so you recognise them instantly and see at a glance which arriving buses will take you there.
